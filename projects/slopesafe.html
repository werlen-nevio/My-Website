SlopeSafe

A platform delivering real-time avalanche danger levels for Swiss ski resorts, with an interactive map, weather forecasts, and official SLF data integration.

Why SlopeSafe

Living in Valais means living next to mountains that do not forgive being underestimated. Avalanche bulletins exist, the SLF publishes excellent data, but the experience of checking the danger before you head out is fragmented across PDFs, regional sites and apps that each tell a different part of the story.

SlopeSafe brings it together: a single map, a single danger level per resort, with the underlying weather and bulletin data one tap away.

How it works

  • Pulls live avalanche bulletins from the official SLF feed
  • Joins them with per-resort weather forecasts
  • Renders the result as an interactive Switzerland-wide map
  • Updates automatically, no manual refresh
The right number on the right map at the right time saves more lives than any feature ever will.

Design

Color is the interface

The European avalanche danger scale runs 1-5 with a fixed color palette. SlopeSafe never overrides those colors. The whole UI orbits around them so the map reads exactly the same as a printed bulletin.

Phone-first

Most sessions happen at 7am at a parking lot before a tour. The phone layout came first, the desktop view second. Touch targets are big, the map is the hero, and everything else can be reached in one tap.
