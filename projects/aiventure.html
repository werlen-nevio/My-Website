AIVenture

A creative daily game where words meet images. Describe an AI-generated picture in your own words, the closer your description matches the AI's interpretation, the higher your score. Built in Silicon Valley as part of the IT Talents program.

The idea

AIVenture is a daily image-describing challenge inspired by Wordle, but instead of guessing a word, you're challenged to describe a daily AI-generated image. Your goal: craft a description that best matches what the AI "thinks" the image is about.

Each day a new picture drops. You write what you see. The AI scores how close your words land to its own interpretation. Simple loop, endless variety, and a different vocabulary battle every morning.

Built in Silicon Valley

AIVenture was developed during the IT Talents program in Silicon Valley, an immersive initiative that sends a hand-picked team of young Swiss developers into the heart of the global tech industry to build a real product under real-world conditions. For a few intense weeks our office was a few miles from the companies whose APIs we were using, the timezone was rough, and the feedback loops were tight.

Our sponsor and mentor on site was Ben Pietrzak, whose industry experience shaped how we scoped the product, prioritised features, and thought about the user experience. Having someone with that background in the room turned a school-style team project into something a lot closer to an actual startup sprint, brutal scope cuts, faster decisions, and a much sharper sense of "what is this game actually for."

How it works

  • A new image is generated and published every day
  • Players type a description in their own words
  • The Gemini-powered backend compares the guess against the image's interpretation
  • A score is returned in real time, the closer the words, the higher the score
Words paint pictures and pictures inspire words.

What I built

I've worked on the frontend side together with the rest of the team. The interface has to make a single text input feel like a game: tight feedback, an obvious scoring loop, and a daily ritual that looks the same every morning so players build the habit. Everything orbits around one image and one input field.

Infrastructure

The whole pipeline runs on Google Cloud Platform. The build pipeline packages the app into a container and pushes it to GCP Container Registry. Deployment is then triggered manually into the running environment, which serves the game on a custom domain.
